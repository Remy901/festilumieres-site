Frequently asked questions

Do I need smart lights?

No. Without lights, every scene plays its sound and on-screen effects. With smart lights, they follow the show as well.

FestiLumieres can't find my lights.

Make sure your device is on the same Wi-Fi network as your lights, then tap « Connecter mes lampes » (connect my lights) and « Relancer la recherche » (search again). On iPhone and Mac, FestiLumieres needs local network access: Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network > turn on FestiLumieres. On a Windows computer, allow FestiLumieres when the firewall asks.

How do I pair a Philips Hue bridge?

In « Mes lampes » (my lights), the bridge shows an « Associer » (pair) button. Press the big round button in the middle of the Hue bridge, then tap « Associer » within 30 seconds. Your Hue lights then appear in the list.

What about Nanoleaf panels?

Hold the Nanoleaf power button for 5 to 7 seconds, until the lights flash, then tap « Associer ».

My Govee lights don't show up.

Govee must allow control over Wi-Fi: in the Govee Home app, open each light's settings and turn on « LAN Control ». Then search again in FestiLumieres.

WiZ and LIFX: anything to do?

No, they are found and controlled automatically, with no account. They just need to have been set up with their own app at least once.

Do my lights go back to how they were?

Yes. Before each scene, FestiLumieres notes each light's state (on or off, color, brightness) and restores it at the end, or as soon as you tap STOP.

Microphone mode doesn't react.

Microphone mode listens to the music playing in the room: allow microphone access when your device asks (otherwise: device settings > FestiLumieres > Microphone). Turn the volume up a little and move closer to the speaker. It needs a few seconds of steady music to find the tempo: the BPM then appears under the STOP button. Music without drums (piano, voice only) gives few flashes.

Which files does « Ma musique » (my music) accept?

MP3, WAV, FLAC and OGG. M4A and AAC files are not supported: for music from a streaming service, use microphone mode instead.

The flashes bother me.

Open Settings (icon at the top right) and turn on « Flashs doux » (reduced flashing): flashing on the screen and on the lights is slowed down and softened. If you feel unwell, tap STOP (or press Escape on a computer).
